# Use a conservative quick-sale price for opportunity ranking

The extension remains read-only and user-triggered, and displays both the lowest Steam listing and the best buy order; candidate cost rate is ranked against the best buy order after estimated Steam fees because the goal is quickly realizable Wallet balance rather than a best-case listing price. Market liquidity is only approximated as active sell listings divided by recent 24-hour sales, so the UI must present it as a heuristic rather than a promised individual sale time; Steam orderbook enrichment is bounded and rate-limited.
